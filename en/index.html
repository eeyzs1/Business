The Twelve Branches

The twelve pillars of a business-school education, mapped to the standard course canon. Each card is a branch folder containing a guide page (sub-branch map + learning path) and four interactive lessons.

BRANCH 01 · ECONOMICS
Economics
The operating system beneath every business decision: choosing under scarcity. Supply and demand, elasticity, market structures, GDP, and inflation.
Sub-branches: Micro / Macro · 4 lessons
BRANCH 02 · ACCOUNTING
Accounting
The language of business. The accounting equation, the three financial statements, and ratio analysis — learn to read a company’s health report.
Sub-branches: Financial / Managerial / Audit · 4 lessons
BRANCH 03 · FINANCE
Finance
The time and the price of money. Time value of money, risk and return, NPV investment decisions, and a map of the financial markets.
Sub-branches: Corporate Finance / Investments / Risk · 4 lessons
BRANCH 04 · MARKETING
Marketing
The science of creating and keeping customers. Value propositions, STP, the 4P mix, and the digital marketing funnel.
Sub-branches: Consumer Behavior / Brand / Digital · 4 lessons
BRANCH 05 · MANAGEMENT
Management
Getting results through others. The four functions, decision biases, motivation theories, teams and leadership.
Sub-branches: Organizational Behavior / Decisions / Leadership · 4 lessons
BRANCH 06 · HUMAN RESOURCES
Human Resources
Managing people as your most important asset. The employee lifecycle, recruiting and selection, performance OKRs/KPIs, compensation and incentives.
Sub-branches: Recruiting / Performance / Compensation / OD · 4 lessons
BRANCH 07 · OPERATIONS & SUPPLY CHAIN
Operations & Supply Chain
The engine that turns promises into deliveries. Process and bottlenecks, quality and lean, the bullwhip effect and supply-chain coordination.
Sub-branches: Production / Quality / Supply Chain / Projects · 4 lessons
BRANCH 08 · STRATEGY
Strategy
Deciding what not to do. Positioning maps, the Five Forces, competitive advantage, and the Business Model Canvas.
Sub-branches: Competitive Strategy / Business Models / Corporate Strategy · 4 lessons
BRANCH 09 · ENTREPRENEURSHIP
Entrepreneurship
A methodology for going from zero to one. Opportunity recognition and validation, the lean-startup loop, business plans, funding and equity.
Sub-branches: Opportunity Validation / Lean Startup / Funding · 4 lessons
BRANCH 10 · BUSINESS ANALYTICS
Business Analytics
Telling business stories with data. Data thinking, statistics and visualization, correlation vs. causation, and decision dashboards.
Sub-branches: Statistics / Business Intelligence / Causal Inference · 4 lessons
BRANCH 11 · INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS
International Business
Doing business across borders. Comparative advantage, trade in practice, the entry-mode ladder, and cultural dimensions.
Sub-branches: International Trade / Multinationals / Cross-Culture · 4 lessons
BRANCH 12 · LAW & ETHICS
Business Law & Ethics
The floor and the guardrails of business. Business entities, contract elements, corporate governance and the agency problem, ESG.
Sub-branches: Business Law / Governance / ESG · 4 lessons

Recommended Learning Paths

Path A · Systematic Survey (recommended)

01 Economics → 02 Accounting → 03 Finance → 04 Marketing → 05 Management → 08 Strategy → 07 Operations → 06 HR → 10 Analytics → 11 International → 12 Law & Ethics → 09 Entrepreneurship (final review)

First build the foundations of where money comes from and how the books are kept, then learn how to sell and how to manage, and finally put it all together.

Path B · Fast Track for Managers

05 Management → 08 Strategy → 02 Accounting → 06 HR → 07 Operations → 10 Analytics

First learn to get results through others, then add the financial and data literacy that decisions require.

Path C · Fast Track for Founders

09 Entrepreneurship → 08 Strategy → 04 Marketing → 03 Finance → 07 Operations → 12 Law & Ethics

Start by validating the idea, then solve positioning, customer acquisition, funding, delivery, and compliance in turn.
