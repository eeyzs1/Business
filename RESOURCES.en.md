# Business Fundamentals · Resources

> *English translation of RESOURCES.md.*

The unified resource library behind the “Further Reading” sections of every branch. Principles: high trust, classics first, better fewer than sloppy.

## Knowledge (canonical textbooks)

- **Principles of Economics — N. Gregory Mankiw**
  The global standard introductory text. Used for: all of Branch 01 (supply & demand, elasticity, market structures, macro).
- **Accounting: The Basis for Business Decisions — John J. Wild** / **The Accounting Game: Basic Accounting Fresh from the Lemonade Stand — Darrell Mullis & Judith Orloff**
  The former is systematic; the latter teaches the accounting equation through a lemonade-stand story. Used for: Branch 02.
- **Corporate Finance — Ross, Westerfield & Jaffe** + **Investments — Bodie, Kane & Marcus**
  The twin pillars of finance. Used for: Branch 03 (time value, NPV, risk & return).
- **Marketing Management — Philip Kotler & Kevin Lane Keller**
  The marketing bible; the original text of STP/4P. Used for: Branch 04.
- **Management — Stephen P. Robbins & Mary Coulter** & **The Practice of Management — Peter F. Drucker**
  The former gives the textbook framework; the latter is the source of the ideas. Used for: Branch 05.
- **Organizational Behavior — Stephen P. Robbins & Timothy A. Judge**
  Motivation, teams, organizational culture. Used for: Branches 05/06.
- **Operations Management — William J. Stevenson** + **The Goal: A Process of Ongoing Improvement — Eliyahu M. Goldratt**
  The latter is the novel-length classic of the Theory of Constraints and the source text for the bottleneck lesson. Used for: Branch 07.
- **Competitive Strategy** & **Competitive Advantage — Michael E. Porter**
  The original texts of the Five Forces and the three generic strategies. Used for: Branch 08.
- **Business Model Generation — Alexander Osterwalder & Yves Pigneur** & **The Lean Startup — Eric Ries** & **Zero to One — Peter Thiel with Blake Masters**
  The Business Model Canvas, the MVP/validation loop, monopoly and innovation. Used for: Branches 08/09.
- **Naked Statistics — Charles Wheelan**
  The friendliest book for building statistical intuition. Used for: Branch 10.
- **International Business: Competing in the Global Marketplace — Charles W. L. Hill**
  The standard international-business text; see the chapter on Hofstede’s cultural dimensions. Used for: Branch 11.
- For business law / corporate governance, the lessons rely on course content plus the corresponding Wikipedia articles (company law, agency problem, ESG); no textbook is assigned yet. Used for: Branch 12.
