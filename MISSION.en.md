# Mission: Business Fundamentals

> *English translation of MISSION.md.*

## Why
The learner wants to build systematic business literacy: to understand how the business world works — how prices form, how companies make money, how organizations coordinate, and how strategy makes trade-offs. The goal is general understanding, not exam preparation or professional certification.

## Success looks like
- When reading a piece of business news (a rate hike, a price increase, an earnings report, an M&A deal), being able to explain the business mechanism behind it
- Being able to use frameworks such as supply & demand, unit economics, and the Five Forces to analyze a real business nearby
- Being able to read a company’s three financial statements and describe its state of health
- Holding your own in conversations with business people, and asking high-quality questions

## Constraints
- Primary study language is Chinese, with a full English mirror of the site under `en/`; the learner prefers concepts expressed through graphics/animation rather than pure text
- Materials must work offline (HTML, zero external dependencies)
- Study time is fragmented; 30–45 minutes per session works best

## Out of scope (for now)
- Professional-qualification depth (CPA/CFA level)
- Mathematical general-equilibrium or econometric derivations
- Operational details specific to one industry (e.g. filling in customs paperwork)

> This mission may be revised as learning progresses — when revising, confirm with the learner and record the change in learning-records.
