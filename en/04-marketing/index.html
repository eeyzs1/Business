Branch 04 · Marketing

Marketing: Creating and Keeping Customers

Every cost a company incurs is won back as revenue at exactly one place: where customers are willing to make the exchange. Marketing is the discipline built around that exchange — what customers actually want, whom we serve, which mix of moves wins them over, and how, in the digital era, customer acquisition becomes a business you can do the math on. This branch’s four lessons take you from “techniques for selling things” to “a system for creating customers”.

Learning Path: Why This Order

The main line: from “why” to “what” to “how to measure it”

Lesson 1 The Essence of Marketing → Lesson 2 STP → Lesson 3 The 4 Ps → Lesson 4 The Digital Funnel

First the mindset — marketing creates customer value; it is not slogan-shouting. Then the choices — resources are limited, so “to whom, saying what” must be answered. Then execution — the 4 Ps translate strategy into moves customers can feel. Finally the dashboard — digital makes every step of acquisition measurable and optimizable.

If you have only one hour: take Lesson 1 to set the mindset, then Lesson 2 to master STP, the most-used framework of all — enough to hold your own in most business discussions. Lessons 3 and 4 lean toward execution and measurement, ideal if you are currently building a product or running operations.
