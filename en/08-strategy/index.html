Branch 08 · Strategy

Strategy

The essence of strategy is choosing what not to do: in a world of limited resources, pick a unique position, build a consistent set of trade-offs around it, and hold it long enough. This branch answers one question — why do some firms earn excess profits for decades while most of their peers merely toil for thin margins?

1Sub-branch Map

Strategy is the “capstone course” of business school: it gathers marketing, finance, organization, and operations into a single question — how does this company intend to win, and keep on winning? The field is usually divided into five sub-domains that answer, in turn: where to fight, how to fight, how the money is collected, whether to expand, and how it all lands.

2Learning Path: Concepts → Industry → the Firm → Monetization

Why learn it in this order

Lesson 1 sets the concepts: strategy = a unique position + trade-offs → Lesson 2 looks up at the industry: the Five Forces decide how attractive its money is → Lesson 3 returns to the firm: what it takes to build moats rivals cannot copy → Lesson 4 closes the loop on cash: turning advantage into money with the Business Model Canvas

First comes the framework of “choice,” then the question of whether profit flows from industry structure or from firm-level advantage, and finally the loop where value converts back into cash — each lesson supplies ammunition for the next.

4Connections to Other Branches

Prerequisite · strongly recommended first

04 Marketing — the birthplace of positioning

STP in marketing (segmentation, targeting, positioning) and strategic positioning are two sides of one coin: marketing asks “which position do we occupy in the customer’s mind,” while strategy asks “which set of activities will deliver on that position.” Learn marketing before strategy and you will keep meeting familiar ideas.

Prerequisite · strongly recommended first

05 Management — the vehicle that carries strategy into action

Strategy executes only through organization and leadership: the four functions — planning, organizing, leading, controlling — are the conveyor belt that turns “choosing what not to do” into daily workflow. This branch’s fifth sub-domain, strategy execution, is fully prepared in the Management branch.

Next up · put strategy to work

09 Entrepreneurship — strategy applied to new ventures

Entrepreneurship is strategy under maximum stress: with no accumulated profits and no incumbent brand, a new venture must find its position, validate a business model, and build its first moat faster than anyone else. Study entrepreneurship after this branch and you will find the lean startup loop full of old friends from these lessons.

Adjacent · two sides of the same coin

07 Operations & Supply Chain / 03 Finance

Operations builds cost leadership into every process step — the execution end of strategy; corporate finance’s valuation and capital budgeting are the arithmetic behind diversification and M&A decisions (the advanced, later part of this branch).
