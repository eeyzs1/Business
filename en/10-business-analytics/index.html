Business Analytics: Let the Data Speak

Businesses generate oceans of data every day: cash registers, advertising consoles, and customer-service systems all record it automatically. But data is not conclusions, and conclusions are not decisions. Business Analytics is the craft of turning data into decisions — first ask the right question, then compute the right numbers, separate causation from mere co-movement, and finally organize the metrics into a one-screen, decision-ready dashboard. This branch’s 4 lessons hand you a portable analytics operating system.

Learning Path: Why This Order

Main line: first learn to “ask the right question” → then to “compute the right numbers” → then to “tell causation from correlation” → finally to “assemble the dashboard”

Lesson 1 Data Thinking → Lesson 2 Descriptive Statistics → Lesson 3 Correlation vs Causation → Lesson 4 Business Intelligence and Dashboards

Tools always come after questions: 90% of analytics failures are not failures of technology but of problem definition and causal misjudgment. So we set up the process first (Lesson 1), then install the guardrails that stop numbers from fooling you (Lesson 2), then pass the most treacherous checkpoint — correlation ≠ causation (Lesson 3), and only then assemble everything into a dashboard (Lesson 4). Predictive tools such as machine learning wait until this foundation is laid.

If you have only one hour: take Lesson 1 to set up the process, then Lesson 3 to dodge the great causation trap — those two steer you around the two most common accidents in business discussions. Lessons 2 and 4 lean toward quantitative fundamentals, well suited to people who read and present reports regularly.
