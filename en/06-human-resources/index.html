Business · Branch 06 · Human Resources

Human Resources

Run people as the organization’s most important asset. This branch follows the thread of the employee lifecycle to make three things clear: how people are selected in (recruiting), how their goals are aligned (performance), and how they are rewarded and energized (compensation). HR is not the payroll department — it is a design discipline for winning with people.

1Sub-branch Map

Human resources management is a big house: every system and every relationship that concerns people lives inside it. This branch’s four lessons follow the employee lifecycle and prioritize the three most widely used system designs — the ones that transfer best to any organization; the remaining sub-branches are marked Advanced · Later, ready to expand into once you finish the branch.

2Learning Path: Why This Order

Follow the employee lifecycle: get the map first, then enter the three systems

Lesson 1 The Employee Lifecycle: An HR Overview → Lesson 2 Recruiting and Selection → Lesson 3 Performance Management: KPIs and OKRs → Lesson 4 Compensation and Incentives

Start with the big-picture map: fit everything HR does into the attract → recruit → onboard → develop → retain → exit loop and see the complete journey a person takes through an organization. Then enter the three systems in lifecycle order: the entrance (recruiting — where people come from), the process (performance — how goals get aligned), and the reward (compensation — how money is paid and hearts are kept). Each lesson maps to a manager’s three most everyday moves: choosing people, deploying people, and motivating people.

3Lessons

  • The Employee Lifecycle: An HR Overview The attract → recruit → onboard → develop → retain → exit loop; from personnel administration to strategic partner; Ulrich’s three pillars. Interactive: lifecycle ring diagram.
  • Recruiting and Selection Talent profiles, recruiting channels, and screening bias; structured vs unstructured interviews; behavioral interviewing and STAR probes. Interactive: STAR interview-question drill.
  • Performance Management: KPIs and OKRs The performance cycle (goals → coaching → appraisal → feedback); SMART goals; KPI vs OKR; continuous feedback. Interactive: goal checkup + KPI/OKR flip cards.
  • Compensation and Incentives 3P pay, total rewards, and fixed-variable mix design; internal equity and external competitiveness; equity and stock options. Interactive: fixed-variable mix simulator.
